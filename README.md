# SupplyShift Game

AI-powered supply-chain strategy game where players forecast hidden demand trends, allocate inventory across regions, set prices, and compete against an adaptive AI agent over twelve months.

## Play the game

**Live Vercel app:** [supplyshift-game-subodh.vercel.app](https://supplyshift-game-subodh.vercel.app)

## How it works

- Plan inventory and pricing for three products across multiple warehouse regions.
- Reveal monthly orders shaped by hidden trends, seasons, prices, and regional demand.
- Compare your decisions with an AI forecasting and inventory agent.
- Track revenue, inventory costs, storage costs, stockouts, and total year-to-date profit.
- Finish the twelve-month challenge with the highest total profit.
